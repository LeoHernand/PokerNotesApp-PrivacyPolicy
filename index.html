Privacy Policy for Poker Notes

Effective Date: 02/11/2026

This privacy policy applies to the Poker Notes app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Leon Hernandez (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad-Supported service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

Personal Information

The Application does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

Local Data Storage

All poker session data, hand histories, analytics, and custom settings created within the Application are stored locally on your device's internal storage. The Service Provider does not transmit, collect, or store this data on any external servers. If you uninstall the Application or clear its data, your saved notes and histories will be permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for the purpose of serving advertisements.

The Application uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

The Application utilizes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to gather and manage necessary user consent for advertising purposes, adhering to regional privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA.

For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Network Access

The Application requests the INTERNET permission solely to communicate with Google AdMob to fetch and display advertisements. No personal app data or poker history is transmitted over this connection.

Children's Privacy

Due to the nature of poker and gambling-related tracking, the Application is not directed to anyone under the age of 18. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
